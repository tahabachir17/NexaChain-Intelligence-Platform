# Executive Recommendations — Supplier Performance

As of 2024-12-01, the spend-weighted supplier portfolio score is **67.52/100**, essentially flat over six months (-0.40 points). Supplier-level movement is material: **121 are improving** and **104 are deteriorating**.

## Decisions

1. Begin replacement due diligence for **5 suppliers** representing **1.1% of annual spend**. Validate substitutes, capacity, switching costs, and exit terms first.
2. Open strategic partnership discussions with **3 suppliers** representing **0.9% of spend**. Tie commitments to measurable service and resilience gains.
3. Put **Deleon-Butler** and the other highest-spend deteriorators on 30/60/90-day recovery plans.
4. Reduce the **77.7% of spend** classified High or Critical through diversification and stronger risk clauses.
5. Recalibrate thresholds quarterly and reconcile product-cost benchmarks with negotiated-price or should-cost data.

## Decision guardrails

- A replacement flag triggers procurement due diligence; it is not an automatic termination.
- Spend indicates exposure and priority, not supplier quality.
- Risk classifications are screening rules and require category-owner review.
- Strategic status should be reviewed monthly and renewed only while score, trend, and risk conditions remain satisfied.
